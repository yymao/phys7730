2. You have $N$ independent measurements $k_1, k_2, \dots, k_N$ that you believe come from
   one particular Poisson distribution with an unknown mean $\lambda$.

   - (a) What is the log-likelihood function $\ln \mathcal{L}(\lambda)$ given this data set $(k_1, k_2, \dots, k_N)$ ?
   - (b) Derive the Maximum Likelihood Estimation for $\lambda$, denoted as $\hat{\lambda}$. Show your steps.
   - (c) The observed Fisher Information for $\hat{\lambda}$ is defined as $ - \frac{d^2}{d\lambda^2} \ln \mathcal{L}(\lambda) \big|_{\lambda = \hat{\lambda}}$.
        Calculate the observed Fisher Information in this case. Show your steps.

   :::{hint}
   The PMF of a Poisson distribution with a mean of $\lambda$ is $P(k) = \frac{\lambda^k e^{-\lambda}}{k!}$
   :::
